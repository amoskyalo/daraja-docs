# Business Buy Goods

This API enables you to pay for goods and services directly from your business account to a till number, merchant store number or Merchant HO.

This API enables you to pay for goods and services directly from your business account to a till number, merchant store number or Merchant HO. You can also use this API to pay a merchant on behalf of a consumer/requester.

The transaction moves money from your MMF/Working account to the recipient's merchant account.
